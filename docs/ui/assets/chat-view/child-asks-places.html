The rules

One place at a time. A task is drawn in one place; pressing another moves it. The line keeps its words and lights where it is.
The ask opens it in place only when it is nowhere else. Already beside or in the panel, the ask is drawn there, and the pill says where.
Every line on the way down has its own group. The review can be in the panel while its Lesson nests inside it.
After the answer. A nest the ask opened shuts; one you opened, a split, the panel and the main view stay where you put them.
The same group in the task view's rail on its started and made lines, and in the head of wherever the task is shown.
Phone: no split; main view and side panel as elsewhere on a phone.
