Why does the inbox strip skip gates

Why does the inbox strip skip gates when two questions are waiting?

The strip lists agent questions first, then command approvals, then gates, and keeps at most two of each kind and three in all. Two questions and one approval fill it, so every gate waits behind the overflow count.
