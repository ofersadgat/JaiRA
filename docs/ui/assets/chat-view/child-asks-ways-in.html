The rules

What you point at decides how deep. The arrow, the line and the start task row: one level. The wait: every task down to the question.
Click is in place; hover offers the places. The group is the same four everywhere — in place, main view, beside, side panel — and lights where the task is.
Every line on the way down works the same. A Lesson inside the review, wherever the review is drawn, has its own arrow, wait and group.
One place at a time. Placed elsewhere, the line shuts its nest and says where (beside); the pill says the same.
A new question opens itself as a click on its wait would (②), when the task is shown nowhere else; already placed, it is drawn there.
Answered, a nest the question opened shuts; what you opened or placed stays. Phone: no hover and no split — a long press on the line or the wait brings the group up.
