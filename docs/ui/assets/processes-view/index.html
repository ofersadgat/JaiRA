Processes as terminals: eight proposals

No Processes view: an agent's background, left-running and escaped processes, and any process from another scope, are terminals (decisions 0028 and 0032). Same session on every board: topodiff on win-desk. Add #dark to the address for the dark theme.

1 · Sections and a scope switch

The Terminals tab grows sections (Background, This turn, Escaped, Ended) and a scope switch at its top; another scope's process opens like any terminal.

2 · Scope chips over one list

Scopes are chips that add to one list (This conversation + Mine); every row is a terminal with a status glyph, and a row opens in place before it is placed.

3 · A strip above the composer

What outlives its call sits in a strip over the composer as small live terminals; a process from another scope can be watched there too.

4 · A Machine tab

The conversation's processes stay in the transcript and the Terminals tab; the rest of the machine is a second tab, Machine, with the same rows.

5 · A table, like top

The Terminals tab opens in the main view as a dense, sortable table across every scope; a row's press opens it as a read-only terminal beside.

6 · A tree

The Terminals tab as a tree: scope, then the process, then what it started (cmd, node, esbuild, conhost), with escapes hung under the parent that really started them.

7 · The card carries it

The background card stays live in its row with its ports, its URL, its load and a mini log; the Terminals tab shrinks to an index, and another scope's process opens from its last line.

8 · A dock below, widened by a ladder

My pick: a split laid below the conversation holds the terminals side by side, and its list ends in a ladder that widens the scope one step at a time.
