Round 2

Round 1's 7 (the card) everywhere, 6's List/Tree toggle with hover places and open-in-line, and a process as a task: its workflow is the built-in Process, whose states are the process states.

1 · The card, live in its row

Round 1's 7 kept: a background command's card stays live in its row (ports, CPU and memory, a load trace, the log, Tell Claude when it prints…, Stop). It is the one card every place shows.

2 · The Terminals tab, List

List mode: hovering a row shows the same three places and … as the card; pressing a row opens it in line, the row becoming the card (here the dev server and postgres).

3 · The Terminals tab, Tree

Tree mode: session, process, children (cmd, node, esbuild, conhost), the escaped node.exe under WmiPrvSE.exe; a hovered node shows the same places, and the escaped one is open in line.

4 · The card in a split

Split pressed on the card: the same card fills a split beside the chat, Split lit on it, on the row in the transcript, and on the row in the Terminals tab.

5 · The card in the main view

Main view pressed: the card takes the main view (the conversation a ‹ away), Main lit on it and on its row in the Terminals tab.

6 · A process is a task

Every process is a task whose workflow is the built-in Process: a column on the board like Session or Planning, its cards' badges are process states, and the sidebar lists processes as task rows.

7 · Inside the Process workflow

Going into the Process column opens the pseudo-workflow: its states as a strip and as lanes, every process task sitting in its current state, its origin on its card.

8 · A process task, opened

Opening a process task opens its task view, and that view is the card: crumbs topodiff › Process › one dev, its Process state and the states it went through, its origin and its parent task.

9 · Process tasks nested in the sidebar

The sidebar's Tasks tab nests each process task under the task that started it, with its Process state on the row; a done parent with a live child stays findable, and an orphan (postgres from Mine) stands alone.

The conversation's processes stay in the transcript and the Terminals tab; the rest of the machine is a second tab, Machine, with the same rows.

5 · A table, like top

The Terminals tab opens in the main view as a dense, sortable table across every scope; a row's press opens it as a read-only terminal beside.

6 · A tree

The Terminals tab as a tree: scope, then the process, then what it started (cmd, node, esbuild, conhost), with escapes hung under the parent that really started them.

7 · The card carries it

The background card stays live in its row with its ports, its URL, its load and a mini log; the Terminals tab shrinks to an index, and another scope's process opens from its last line.
