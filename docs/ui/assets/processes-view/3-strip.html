3 · A strip above the composer

  • Background processes leave the transcript: the row says running in background · 4m · :8145 · in the strip below, and the strip over the composer holds a small live terminal for each, as the activity strip holds the turn.
  • Each tile is a terminal folded small: glyph, program, ports and Stop, its status under them; a press opens it in a place of your choosing. The left-running one says why it has no output; the escaped one is amber and names its parent (WmiPrvSE.exe) and the call it came from.
  • Watch a process… picks from another scope (here Mine: postgres, followed from its log file with history) and pins it in the strip. Unreadable ones say why in the picker.
  • An ended background process stays one turn as a red tile (Claude was told), then leaves. The strip's head carries Stop all and the two settings, lightly.
  • Cost: it takes 230px of the conversation's height while anything runs; folded, it is one line.
