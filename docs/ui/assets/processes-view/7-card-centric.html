7 · The card carries it

  • The transcript is the view: a background card never folds while its process lives, and it grows a second line: ports as links (localhost:8145 ↗), pid, CPU, memory and a load trace. Tell Claude when it prints… turns 0032's wait_process until output into a person's verb.
  • Left running and escaped are one-line cards in their rows, each with why (no output kept; started through WMI) and the full title bar's Stop, places and …. An ended background process puts its exit and last line under its row with Claude was told.
  • The Terminals tab is an index: one line per process, and Open a process on this machine… for every other scope. Opened, postgres is a terminal in a split, listed under Watching.
  • Cost: a long conversation scatters its live processes up the transcript; the index is how you find them again.
