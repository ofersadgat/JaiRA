1 · Sections and a scope switch

  • The tab keeps 0028's anatomy and only grows sections: Background (what outlives its call: started with run_in_background, or left running and adopted), This turn (foreground, folds on exit), Escaped (amber, its own section, never mixed in), Ended (newest first; a background end that Claude was told about stays tinted).
  • A scope switch at the tab's top: Conversation is the default; JaiRA, Mine, Group, System and All swap the list for that scope's processes in the same rows. Opening one (here postgres from Mine, placed in a split) is a terminal like any other, read-only: its facts, its log file's history and then what it writes from the moment it was opened.
  • The left-running terminal says why it has no output and offers what can be read instead (its console, followed from now).
  • Cost: the switch replaces the list, so you cannot see the session and Mine together.
