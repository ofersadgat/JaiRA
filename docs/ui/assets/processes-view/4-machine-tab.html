4 · A Machine tab

  • Two questions, two tabs: Terminals is this conversation's (as 0028 drew it, plus background, left running, escaped); Machine is everything else, scoped by one menu (Mine by default; JaiRA, Group, System, All).
  • Machine's rows are the Terminals tab's rows: a followed process is a card with its last two lines and where they come from (log file · history, console); an unreadable one says why in one line; the rest are compact rows. A press opens any of them as a terminal on the panel's stack.
  • In the transcript each kind has its own card: background stays live with its ports, left running is dashed and says why there is no output, escaped is amber with its parent and the call it came from, and an end is a note the agent also got.
  • Cost: one more tab, and Mine never sits beside the conversation's own.
