The permission board · try the row treatments

The runtime view with three levels: a resource, its accesses, and the tools on each access. Switch treatments to compare them, turn the access icons on or off, and play with it. Click a resource or an access to open it. Drag a chip to another column in its own row. A resource's chip moves every access it's tagged with; an access's chip moves all its tools; a tool's chip moves only that tool. Tools that disagree make a MIXED chip on their access, parked in the strictest column. Everything you move is marked ⚡, given this session.
